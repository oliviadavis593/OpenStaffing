# Code of Conduct

OpenStaffing exists to support nurses and the patients they care for. We want working on it to
feel welcoming for everyone, including people who have never contributed to open source before.

## Our standards

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Make room for non-engineers. Nurses, organizers, and patients are experts in what matters here.
- Be accurate and honest about the data, including its limits.
- Respect privacy. Never share personal information about nurses, patients, or anyone else.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, including on hospital staff, executives, or public officials.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or events.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, chat, and project events, including Chi
Hack Night breakout sessions. When we meet at Chi Hack Night, its
[code of conduct](https://chihacknight.org/code-of-conduct) applies too.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) (it works for conduct reports too) or
by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove
content or contributors that break these standards.
